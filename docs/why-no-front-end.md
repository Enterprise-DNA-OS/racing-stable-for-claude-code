# Why there is no front end

Prism is a database with a subscription. The records underneath it are ordinary: horses, owners, shares, treatments, races, trackwork, charges and invoices. What you pay for, module by module, is the layer on top that lets people who do not write queries get at them: forms, calendars, owner screens.

That layer used to be the whole product, because talking to a database was hard. It is not hard any more. Open this folder in Claude Code, describe what you want, and it runs the right query and explains the answer. Ask a question the dashboard never had a report for and you still get an answer.

## What you gain

- **Better answers.** Which horses are entered for a race they cannot run in? What has this horse cost its owners against what it has won? You ask in your own words.
- **No module or per-horse pricing.** Every horse and every owner can be in the records. The bill does not grow with the stable.
- **Your data in your Postgres.** Plain tables. Back them up, query them from anything, leave any time.
- **Your training agreement, as written.** Your rates, your properties, your house rules, changed by asking.

## What a screen gives that this does not

- **An owner portal.** Owners here get updates and invoices by email from drafts the office sends. A portal where owners log in and see their horses is the most common thing a stable adds, and Enterprise DNA builds it on the same records.
- **A phone screen in the barn.** Strappers and riders here tell the foreman, who records treatments and work, or speak it into Claude Code. A one-tap treatment form at the stall is a customisation.
- **Offline use.** It runs where Claude Code runs.
- **Racing Australia's daily file.** Prism reads Racing Australia's registration file every morning. Here, a person lodges and records; bringing the file in is a customisation.

## Who this fits

A trainer or stable office that already uses Claude Code, or would rather ask than learn another screen. If your owners expect a portal on day one, have it built before you switch.
